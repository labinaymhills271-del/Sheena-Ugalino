A few words about me

Hi! I'm Sheena F. Ugalino, but you can call me Shane or Ash. I'm a 13-year-old Grade 7 student at Mapurao Integrated Learning School in the Philippines.

I love learning new things and exploring my creativity. Some of my favorite things include listening to music, singing, dancing, fashion, writing, editing, cooking, and DIY projects.

School has also given me opportunities to discover my strengths. I've participated in journalism, mathematics competitions, storytelling, pageants, and different student organizations.

I believe being a student isn't only about grades. It's about experiences, challenges, friendships, creativity, and constantly becoming a better version of yourself.
